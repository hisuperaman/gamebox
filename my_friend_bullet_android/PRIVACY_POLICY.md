# Privacy Policy: My Friend Bullet (Android app)

**Effective date:** 21 November 2026
**Developer:** hisuperaman ([hisuperaman.me](https://hisuperaman.me))
**App:** My Friend Bullet (`com.hisuperaman.myfriendbullet`)

## Summary

My Friend Bullet does not collect, share or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline and has no internet access.

## What the app stores

The app keeps a few game settings **only on your device**, so they're still there the next time you play:

- your high score
- whether sound is on or off
- your chosen fire mode (single shot or automatic)

This information never leaves your device. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not ask for or use any Android permissions, including internet access.
- It has no accounts, sign-in or player names.
- It has no ads, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- It does not access your contacts, photos, files, location, camera or microphone.
- It contains no in-app purchases.

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Website version

This policy covers the Android app only. The browser version of the game on the gamebox website has an online leaderboard, where you choose a player name that is shown publicly with your score.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
